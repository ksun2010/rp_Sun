Procedure

Using a numbered list, describe your procedure (including pictures)


Step

Description

Image

1

  • Set-up the algal mother culture in order to cultivate the algae.
  • Mix fresh spring water, alga-grow, Chlorella Vulgaris, and a beaker.
  • Place the beaker under a light source for 24 hours to allow the algae to grow.

2

  • Create the test culture. Let the algal mother culture grow for 24 hours before proceeding.
  • Split the mother culture into five test cultures, covering each with a different color filter (red, blue, green, yellow, and clear).
  • Place the test cultures under a light source for 24 hours to allow the algae to grow.

3

  • After 24 hours, take the covering off each test culture and measure with a Dissolved Oxygen Probe.
  • Measure each for ten seconds and record data in the Vernier Graphical Analysis software.
  • Connect a computer to the probe.

4

  • Monitor the trend of dissolved oxygen levels in each test culture over time.
  • Values are automatically put into the table in the Graphical Analysis software.
  • If the trend increases, the algae is producing oxygen. If the trend decreases, the algae is consuming oxygen.

5

  • Repeat the experiment multiple times to ensure accuracy and reliability of the results.
